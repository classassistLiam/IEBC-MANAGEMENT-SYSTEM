Secure staff workspace concept

Choose your operational entry point

Select the access tier assigned to your authorised account. Your permissions should be verified by the server, not by this page.

Tier 01 · Field operations

County & sub-county officers

For authorised staff submitting assigned station or local-area data and viewing only the records within their permitted scope.

Tier 02 · Regional tally

Regional tally centre

For designated regional reviewers to monitor submissions, flag inconsistencies, and review records within their assigned region.

Tier 03 · National oversight

National tally & system admin

For specifically authorised national-level accounts. Oversight access should be read-only unless a separately authorised action is granted.

Access tiers shown here are a proposed application design, not a statement of official IEBC staffing structure or permission policy.

🔐 Account protection

One identity.
Two verification steps.

Use the sign-in panel to select an access tier and enter your credentials. A production backend should validate the password, return a server-defined challenge question, and validate the answer without revealing stored secrets to the browser.

  • Role and region checked on every server request
  • Challenge answer verified on the server
  • Session expiry and sign-out controls
  • Audit trail for privileged actions
  • Admin privileges scoped to approved tasks

Security note: Do not put usernames, passwords, challenge answers, or shared secrets in HTML/JavaScript. Store account configuration in Apps Script Properties or an appropriately secured server-side store; use salted password hashes and rate limits for production.
